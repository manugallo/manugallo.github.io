About

Manu Gallo

I'm a 3D sculptor working in ZBrush and 3ds Max. I grew up on 80s and 90s imagery — cartoons, heavy metal covers, arcade cabinets and toy shelves — and that is still what I like to build.

I work on original characters, fan art and custom commissions, and I keep improving every model: better shapes, better splits, cleaner files for printing.
